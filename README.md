# Hi, I'm LazyLlama 🦙🍹

A relaxed hobby developer who builds **cozy, lightweight things for games**, mostly World of Warcraft addons for now. I take it slow, sip something cold, and try to make games feel a little warmer and more personal.

## 🌅 Currently brewing

### [Journeykeeper — Echoes of Your Journey](https://github.com/LazyLlamaDev/Journeykeeper)
A memory and journal addon for **WoW Forever** (the 1–60 reimagining, launching Nov 4, 2026).
It quietly remembers your firsts, deaths, fights and the places that mattered, then echoes them back when you return.

- 📖 Book-like journal: Timeline, Firsts, Chapters, Fights & Records
- ✨ Echoes: small contextual memory reminders
- 🗺️ Real zoomable Kalimdor & Eastern Kingdoms journey map
- ⚙️ Everything configurable, and your data is never lost across updates

`/jkp` · `/journeykeeper`

## 🧉 How I like to build
- **Quiet by default.** No spam, no popups in combat.
- **Local first.** Your data stays on your PC.
- **Free.** No in-game donation asks, ever.
